Chennai Campus

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Chennai Campus is located in Chennai and mainly focuses on engineering and technology education. It offers courses like B.Tech, M.Tech, and PhD in areas such as Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Data Science, and Electronics, and the university is ranked among the top universities in India in NIRF rankings.

Popular Courses: IT, MBA, Electronics
Facilities: Digital Library, Placement Cell
Focus: Career Development
