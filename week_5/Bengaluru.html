Bengaluru Campus

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Bengaluru Campus is a private university campus located in Bengaluru, established in 2002 and accredited with NAAC A++ grade. It offers programs like B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, and PhD in fields such as Computer Science, AI, Electronics, Robotics, Data Science, and Electrical Engineering.

Popular Courses: Robotics, CSE, ECE
Facilities: Coding Labs, Innovation Centres
Focus: Industry Collaboration
