Kochi Campus

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Kochi Campus is located in Kochi and is famous for its medical and health science programs. It offers courses like MBBS, B.Sc Nursing, Pharmacy, Allied Health Sciences, and postgraduate medical programs, and it is connected with the well-known Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences, one of the major hospitals in India.

Popular Courses: MBA, Commerce, Finance
Facilities: Seminar Halls, Library
Focus: Industry Training
