Anime Power Systems

A power level system is used in anime to measure and compare the strength of characters in a structured way.
It helps viewers understand how powerful a character is, how they grow over time, and how they rank against others in the same universe.
These systems are often based on factors like physical strength, energy, skills, experience, and limitations.
Power level systems make battles more meaningful by setting clear expectations and showing progression through training, strategy, and sacrifice.
